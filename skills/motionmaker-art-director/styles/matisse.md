# Matisse paper cut-outs  (`matisse`)

**Rating:** 2 - reads as a joyful cut-paper collage; the algae are simpler than Matisse's
**Era:** late 1940s-1950s. **Font:** fraunces 700 (OFL, bundled by `lib.mjs setup fonts`).
**Good for:** joyful brands, culture and education, kids and family, summer campaigns, anything that wants warmth and rhythm.

## Recognisable cues (all three must be visible in the poster frame)
- shapes cut from gouache-painted paper: cobalt, vermilion, lemon, emerald, pink, black
- seaweed/algae forms with lobes, stars, confetti, on coloured paper rectangles
- no outlines; visible brush texture in the paper

## Palette
#F3EBDD wall, #1F3FA6 cobalt, #E2482B vermilion, #F4C434 lemon, #1F8A5B emerald, #EE9AA8 pink, #1B1B1B black, #7FB3E6 sky

## Main action (the one hero move)
the papers slide into a collage, then the headline is cut out of a black strip by a wavy scissor edge.

## Motif loops (what lives in the picture; every period divides the clip length, so loops close at the seam)
1. the algae sway from their base like seaweed
2. the stars turn slowly
3. confetti cut-outs drift on closed paths

## Pitfalls (found while building it)
- the algae need big alternating lobes; a uniform zigzag reads as a twisted rope (fixed during build)

## Known weaknesses
algae shapes are generated, not drawn: they lack the hand's surprise; consider the user's own cut-outs for hero work.

## Making it yours
Keep the cues and the palette; change the headline, the timing (`e.enter` start/duration in the style file) and the seed (`SEED=`), never the motif mechanics per frame. Brand colours: only where the style allows an accent (the red in Bauhaus/Constructivism, the seal in ukiyo-e); do not repaint a style in brand colours or it stops being that style.
