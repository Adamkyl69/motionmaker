# Art styles

Movements and long-dead artists only (public domain), drawn by our own code (`motionmaker/templates/styles/<id>.js`). Living artists' and studios' signature styles (Ghibli, Kurzgesagt, Vox, Kusama...) are deliberately not offered.
Ratings judge OUR implementation: 3 = recognisable at a glance, ready to use; 2 = usable, read its weaknesses; 1 = experimental.
Use: `TEMPLATE=style-scene STYLE=<id> bash $MOTIONMAKER_SKILLS/motionmaker/scripts/scaffold.sh <slug> "<headline or ->" W H fps secs - - -`. Pick with `node scripts/styles.mjs frames "<headline>" --styles a,b,c`.

| Style | Rating | Era | Good for | Main action | Motifs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bauhaus](bauhaus.md) `bauhaus` | ★★★ | 1919-1933 | product launches, design and architecture, data, anything that wants order and confidence | a heavy black bar slides in on a diagonal and the headline rides on it | the black pointer bar sweeps around the red circle like a dial; the 3x3 grid recolours in steps; concentric arcs breathe out of the circle |
| [Van Gogh night sky](van-gogh.md) `van-gogh` | ★★★ | 1889 era | stories, launches with a dreamy or romantic tone, night/sleep/space products, art and culture | the headline is painted in, stroke by stroke (strokes sampled from the type's own shape), in chrome and lemon | the sky turns; star and moon halos pulse outward in rings of strokes; the cypress sways from its base |
| [Russian Constructivism](constructivism.md) `constructivism` | ★★★ | 1919-1930s | announcements, calls to action, launches with urgency, protest-energy campaigns, bold data claims | the wedge thrusts from the upper left into the circle | the circle turns with its radial marks; a marquee band repeats the headline along the bottom; the halftone field breathes |
| [Seurat pointillism](seurat.md) `seurat` | ★★★ | 1880s | leisure, travel, outdoors, slow and luminous moods, premium lifestyle | the headline condenses out of dots (ink, deep blue and a few vermilion), on a soft cream band so it stays readable | about 15% of the dots twinkle between two hues, stepped at 8 fps; the sailboat drifts and bobs on the river; the sun's halo of dots brightens outward |
| [Matisse paper cut-outs](matisse.md) `matisse` | ★★ | late 1940s-1950s | joyful brands, culture and education, kids and family, summer campaigns, anything that wants warmth and rhythm | the papers slide into a collage, then the headline is cut out of a black strip by a wavy scissor edge | the algae sway from their base like seaweed; the stars turn slowly; confetti cut-outs drift on closed paths |
| [Ukiyo-e woodblock print](ukiyo-e.md) `ukiyo-e` | ★★ | Edo period, 1700s-1850s | travel, Japan-related subjects, craft and heritage brands, calm-but-dramatic stories, ocean/water topics | the cartouche unrolls top to bottom and the headline is printed into it | the big wave's crest curls and its foam claws reach; the lower swell rolls through three bands; the boats ride it; clouds drift past Fuji, fading in from the wave's edge |
